## Doing

### Getting to know my new home :puerto_rico:

I moved to San Juan, Puerto Rico in June of 2025! Since then I and my family have spent our time getting to know the island, enjoying the beach, scuba diving, and exploring our neighboring islands. Moving here has been a goal for a very long time and having done has been incredibly enriching. Learning about and sharing in Puerto Rican and Caribbean culture has been a blast and learning a new language is a wonderful challenge.

### Homelab

My homelab currently consists of 11 ThinkCentre Tiny PCs running Debian and k3s, a QNAP NAS, and Mikrotik routing and switching. The lab is mostly a way to learn and experiment with new technology, but I also operate services like [Jellyfin](https://github.com/jellyfin/jellyfin), [Mealie](https://github.com/mealie/mealie), and [Uptime Kuma](https://github.com/uptimekuma/uptimekume) for myself and my family.

## Reading

I track my reading on [BookHive](https://bookhive.buzz/profile/benjamin.bckr.me), an ATProto bookshelf application similar to Goodreads (an Amazon property). I tend to read broadly, but gravitate towards science fiction, epic fantasy, and narrative histories. I especially enjoy [Iain M. Banks](https://bookhive.buzz/authors/Iain%20M.%20Banks), [Alastair Reynolds](https://bookhive.buzz/authors/Alastair%20Reynolds), [Anthony Kaldellis](https://bookhive.buzz/authors/Anthony%20Kaldellis), [Ursula Le Guin](https://bookhive.buzz/authors/Ursula%20K.%20Le%20Guin).
